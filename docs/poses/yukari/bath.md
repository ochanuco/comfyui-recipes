The costume is a white oversized t-shirt, dolphin shorts and a towel
around the neck; `wet hair` and `steam` were tried and dropped -- they
spread onto the skin as droplets and a wet, see-through shirt, which read
as too wet. The towel is the only thing that says bath. The legs are bare:
`bath` swaps the recipe's pantyhose block for `bare legs, barefoot` and
appends `pantyhose, black pantyhose, shoes` to the negative, since the
shared negative only ever banned pantyhose defects, never the garment.

`knee up` with `outstretched leg` is the seat; `knees up` alone (both knees,
三角座り) rated fine but the user wanted one leg out. `hands on own leg`,
`both hands` and `holding own leg` put both hands on the same leg -- with
`hand on own leg` alone the free hand wandered to the floor and the
gesture read as resting, not rubbing. `looking down` replaces `looking at
viewer`: she is looking at the leg.

There is no cream, jar or lotion in the prompt. `lotion bottle` became a
glass jar and `cream, blue jar` a canister, and both pulled a floor and
tiles into the frame; the user asked for no objects, so the rubbing hands
carry the whole idea. The background stays the recipe's `simple
background, grey background`, although `on floor` still draws floorboards
on some seeds.
