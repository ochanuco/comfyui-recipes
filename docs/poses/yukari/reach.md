# reach

長座体前屈 she is too stiff to finish: seated, legs out along the long side,
arms reaching short of her feet, knees refusing to stay down.

## What the block holds

`(sitting:1.5)` outranks `(stretching:1.3)`. Rank, not weight, is the lever
here -- with the stretch tag leading, the model draws a standing forward bend
and the seat disappears entirely, and raising `sitting` underneath it does not
buy it back.

`(from side:1.4)` is the camera. A long sit seen from the front or from above
has no silhouette: the legs point at the lens and the exercise reads as a
crouch. The side view is the same fix `situp` needed, for the same reason.

`(knees up:1.2)` is the stiffness, and it is the one tag that must stay eased.
Weighted, the legs fold under her and the long sit goes with them.

The thighs are framed by `thigh focus` on the near leg plus the landscape
canvas, which is what keeps the extended legs in frame instead of cropped at
the shin.

## Guards

`stretching` brings gym kit with it exactly as `sit-up` does; the same
sportswear guard answers it, released for the shod costumes. The split guard
is this pose's own: a seated stretch is one weight away from a straddle, and
`legs together` alone does not hold it.

The soles face the camera at the far end of the legs, so the toe guard the
delivery adds is already needed a pass earlier.

## Not settled

The framing is stable; the posture inside it is not. Most seeds return a
knees-up sit or an all-fours reach rather than the failing forward bend.
